Dry Oar Rafting Co.

Our mission is to provide exhilarating, safe, and unforgettable white water rafting experiences that connect people with the raw beauty of nature. We believe in adventure, environmental stewardship, and creating memories that last a lifetime. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced rafter, our team is dedicated to guiding you through the rush of the rapids with absolute confidence and joy.

History

Founded in 2010 by a group of passionate river guides, Dry Oar Rafting Co. started with just two rafts and a dream to share the most thrilling rapids with adventure seekers. Over the years, we have expanded our routes, upgraded our fleet, and trained dozens of certified experts. Today, we are proud to be recognized as a premier eco-tourism rafting company, serving thousands of happy adventurers every single year.
