Half-Day Family Splash

Perfect for families and beginners. This 3-hour journey features gentle Class II rapids and spectacular canyon scenery. Includes a light snack and professional guide instruction.

The Big Drop Extreme

Designed for true adrenaline junkies. A full-day excursion conquering Class IV and V rapids, including the famous 'Devil's Teeth' drop. Previous rafting experience is recommended.

Full-Day Wilderness Escape

Immerse yourself completely in nature. Spend 7 hours navigating scenic canyons, swimming in calm pools, and conquering Class III rapids. A full riverside buffet lunch is provided.
